Green AI: Predictive Workload Scheduling and Quantization for Carbon-Aware Cloud Computing

Research Internship Output

Abstract

This study demonstrates how cloud computing infrastructure can significantly reduce its carbon footprint and electricity consumption through a tripartite approach. First, a predictive model forecasts workload surges to enable proactive resource allocation. Second, a carbon-aware scheduler delays non-urgent, flexible compute tasks until the energy grid's carbon intensity is low. Finally, inference optimization via model quantization reduces the per-execution energy cost of AI workloads without substantial loss of accuracy. Our results, visualized in this dashboard, prove that combining these techniques yields a measurable reduction in both total energy used and estimated CO₂ emissions compared to a standard, reactive baseline.
